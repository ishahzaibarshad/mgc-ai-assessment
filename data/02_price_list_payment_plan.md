# MGC Aurora Heights — Price List & Payment Plan

## Location Premiums
- Floors 13–19: +4%
- Floors 20–22: +7%
- Corner: +3%
- Margalla-facing: +6%
- Premiums are cumulative.
- Therefore, a Margalla-facing corner unit on floor 15 carries +13% over the applicable base price.
